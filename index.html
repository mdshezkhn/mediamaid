FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. MediaMaid processes everything locally in your browser using WebCodecs and WebAssembly. Your files never leave the device.

Which watermarks can it remove?

The sparkle-style marks from Gemini, Veo/Flow, Sora, Kling, Grok, Hailuo, Luma, Runway and PixVerse — plus any logo or caption bar you can draw a box around.

Is there a file-size limit?

No hard limit. The only ceiling is your device's memory — typical phones handle hundreds of megabytes comfortably.

Does it cost anything?

No. MediaMaid is free, with no account and no watermark on outputs.
